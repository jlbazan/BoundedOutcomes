

The first version of this material was prepared for the course “Regression and Latent Variable
Models for Bounded Outcomes,” taught during the XLVIII Jornadas Nacionales de Estadística,
organized by the Sociedad Chilena de Estadística and the Instituto de Estadística de la Universidad
de Valparaíso, held in Valparaíso, Chile, on October 21–22, 2026. 

Are available the following files:

- ResumenJNE2026.pdf  Summary of the Minicourse
- Boundedbook.pdf     Ebook associated for the Minicourse
- IntroductionSlides.pdf Slides associated to the Introduction of the Ebook
- BoundedContinuosRegressionSlides.pdf Slides associated to Chapter 2 of the Ebook
- BoundedCountRegressionSlides.pdf Slides associated to Chapter 3 of the Ebook
- BoundedIRTSlides.pdf Slides associated to Chapter 4 of the Ebook
- BoundedCDMSlides.pdf Slides associated to Chapter 5 of the Ebook

R codes 
- BoundedContinuosRegression.R Codes associated to Chapter 2 of the Ebook
- BoundedCountRegression.R Codes associated to Chapter 3 of the Ebook
- BoundedIRT.R Slides Codes associated to Chapter 4 of the Ebook

Application1 is a folder with codes and data associated to Chapter 5 of the Ebook
